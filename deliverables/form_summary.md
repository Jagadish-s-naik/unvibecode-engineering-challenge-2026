In interactive terminals, CustomerDashboardRenderer tracks cursor displacement via relative ANSI CUU sequences based solely on its own line count. When the server streams non-dashboard events (such as the interim results URL), cli.py prints them directly to stdout, pushing the physical cursor down by 3+ rows without notifying the renderer. On the subsequent refresh, clear() moves up by the stale row count, permanently erasing the interim results announcement while leaving the upper lines of the prior dashboard frozen as orphaned ghost headers. Tested deterministically (100% hit rate across 3 trials) on UnvibeCode 0.4.0 across 5 public repositories.
